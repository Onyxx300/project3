You play as a turtle trying to evade an angry triangle, all the while grabbing 
cash. You have three tries to get as much cash as possible before the triangle 
gets you. But be careful! Every time it passes, it takes 100 cash away from 
you! How long can you survive for? And how much cash can you salvage?

CONTROLS:
←↑↓→: Move
Esc: Quit

RULES:
-Coins reward you 10 points each
-The triangle deducts 100 points
-After the triangle hits you thrice, it's game over
