You are a caterpillar with a humongous appetite, and your mission is to get as
much fruit as you possibly can! But be careful: you can hit your head on the 
walls, or accidentally give yourself a venomous bite! How much fruit can you
get?

CONTROLS:
←↑↓→: Move

RULES:
-Each fruit is worth 2 points and increases caterpillar speed
-Running into yourself or a wall results in a game over
-Game window is twice as large as regular Hungry Caterpillar
